# Credits

The sprite sheets in this repository were edited by **GG Vibes Games** (https://gg-vibes-games.itch.io) from the
Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) parts below, which were put together with the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator

Each part may be used under any one of the licences listed for it. Because some parts are CC-BY-SA only
(frock coat, formal shirt, cane, parted and long hair), the edited sheets are shared under CC-BY-SA 3.0.

## Which parts are in which sheet

- **Protagonist** (`lpc_protagonist_walk.png`, `lpc_protagonist_candle.png`, `lpc_protagonist_candle_front.png`):
  Body (male), Head: Human Male, Face: Neutral with eyes (male), Hair: Parted, Shirt: Collared/Formal Longsleeve,
  Jacket: Frock coat (charcoal), Legs: Formal Pants, Feet: Revised Shoes.
  Sitting (`lpc_protagonist_sit.png`): the same, but Shirt: Longsleeve 2 Buttoned and, as the coat, Cardigan (recoloured charcoal).
- **Blinks** (every `*_idle.png` and the sitting sheet): Face: Closed (male; elderly).
- **Old man** (`lpc_old_man_walk.png`):
  Body (male), Head: Human Male Elderly, Face: Neutral with eyes (elderly), Nose: Elderly, Hair: Plain,
  Shirt: Collared/Formal Longsleeve, Jacket: Frock coat (walnut), Legs: Formal Pants, Feet: Revised Shoes, Cane.
  His green eyeshade is not an LPC part; it was drawn for the game.
- **Old woman** (`lpc_old_woman_walk.png`, `lpc_old_woman_shawl.png`, `old_woman_rocking.png`, `old_woman_rocking_look.png`):
  Body (female), Head: Human Female Elderly, Face: Neutral with eyes (elderly), Nose: Elderly, Hair: Long,
  Shirt: Longsleeve (female), Dress: Bodice (maroon), Legs: Plain skirt (thin), Hat: Kerchief (white; recoloured into a shawl).
